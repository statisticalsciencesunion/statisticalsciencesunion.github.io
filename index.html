§ 01

About

The SSU exists to represent all undergraduate students enrolled in a major, specialist, or minor program offered by the Department, as well as students taking any STA or ACT-designated course.

We run workshops on statistics, from hands-on skill-building sessions to hackathons and case competitions. We host conversations with faculty and alumni working in academia, industry, and public health, through panels, tea with professors, and networking nights. We host socials that bring the program together outside the classroom. We maintain study resources our members actually use, with regular study sessions built into the year.

If you study statistics - whether you are in a specialist, major, minor, or enrolled in a DoSS course - you are welcome and encouraged to join!.

§ 01

About

Founded by undergraduates in the Department of Statistical Sciences, the SSU exists because no club at UofT was speaking directly to statistics students about what we love and what we struggle with.

We run workshops on R, Python, and statistical machine learning. We host conversations with faculty and alumni working in academia, industry, and public health. We maintain study resources our members actually use, and we make space for the kind of mentorship that gets undergraduates through the harder courses.

The union is run entirely by students, for students. If you study statistics, want to study statistics, or just like sitting next to people who do, you belong here.
